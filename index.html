01Professional Profile

Counsel grounded in court, trained for the negotiating table.

Holding a Bachelor of Laws (LL.B Hons.) from the University of the Punjab and a BS in Pakistan Studies from Allama Iqbal Open University, Zill E Rukh Mushtaq serves as Legal Representative at an Autonomous Organization under the Ministry of Interior, Government of Pakistan — regularly appearing before the Hon'ble Lahore High Court — alongside a role as Associate Partner at FARZIL LAW.

He now leads FARZIL LAW | Centre for Dispute Resolution (FLCDR) as its Founder & Director, and is Gazette Notified and enrolled as an Accredited Mediator by the Ministry of Law and Justice, Government of Pakistan.

His alternative dispute resolution credentials are international in scope: a triple-accredited Civil/Commercial Mediator through ADR ODR International (recognised by IMI, CMC UK, and SIMI), a Level 1 Accredited Mediator of the Singapore International Mediation Institute, and a qualified Associate Arbitrator. He holds memberships of the Chartered Institute of Arbitrators (UK), Young ICCA, the American Bar Association, the American Society of International Law, and WIPO ADR Young.

His research on integrating artificial intelligence into ADR — published in an HEC-recognised policy journal — argues for a human-centred statutory framework where technology advises and people decide.
