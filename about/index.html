Profile

Accredited Mediator & Associate Arbitrator

Holding a Bachelor of Laws (LL.B Hons.) from the University of the Punjab and a BS in Pakistan Studies from Allama Iqbal Open University, Zill E Rukh Mushtaq is a Lahore-based legal professional and Associate Partner at FARZIL LAW. He now leads FARZIL LAW | Centre for Dispute Resolution (FLCDR) as its Founder & Director, and is Gazette Notified and enrolled as an Accredited Mediator by the Ministry of Law and Justice, Government of Pakistan.

His alternative dispute resolution credentials are international in scope: a triple-accredited Civil/Commercial Mediator through ADR ODR International (recognised by IMI, CMC UK, and SIMI), a Level 1 Accredited Mediator of the Singapore International Mediation Institute, and a qualified Associate Arbitrator. He holds memberships of the Chartered Institute of Arbitrators (UK), Young ICCA, the American Bar Association, the American Society of International Law, and WIPO ADR Young — as well as young-practitioner memberships with ICSID, Young SIAC (YSIAC), the LCIA’s Young International Arbitration Group, and CIICA’s Young Arbitration Group.

He heads the ADR Wing of the Youth Advocacy Forum, where he also sits on the Central Executive Committee, and serves as Chapter Head (Lahore) of AIPC — the ADR ODR International × ICDRL Peacemakers Community.

Independently of those roles, he was appointed in October 2026 to the adjudication panel of Teen Mediation International (TMI), United States, judging the initial and semi-final rounds of its Global Online Mediation & Negotiation Tournament — assessing negotiation and settlement craft among teams drawn from more than twenty nationalities.

In November 2026 he was guest speaker for Day 01 of the Legal Hub Community’s two-day workshop Dispute Resolution 2.0, streamed live — leading a session on foundations, mediation practice and ADR vs. ODR, from the stages of a mediation to the enforcement of mediated settlements. He is next set to adjudicate national arbitration and mediation moot competitions, including Arbitra’26.

His research on integrating artificial intelligence into ADR — The Digital Mediator, published in an HEC-recognised policy journal — argues for a human-centred statutory framework where technology advises and people decide. He has also written on international arbitration in the Pakistan Law Journal, and two further works on AI and the Singapore Convention are expected in December 2026, in the Himalayan Politics Journal and the Pakistan Law Journal.
