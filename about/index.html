Profile

Accredited Mediator & Associate Arbitrator

Zill E Rukh Mushtaq is a Lahore-based legal professional, accredited mediator and associate arbitrator. He holds an LL.B. (Hons.) from the University of the Punjab, Lahore, and a second bachelor’s degree in Pakistan Studies from Allama Iqbal Open University, Islamabad.

He is a Gazette Notified Accredited Mediator with the Ministry of Law and Justice, Government of Pakistan; an internationally accredited civil and commercial mediator (IMI, CMC UK and SIMI Level 1); and an Associate Arbitrator certified by ADR ODR International Ltd (UK). As Founder & Director of FARZIL LAW | Centre for Dispute Resolution (FLCDR), his work focuses on ADR, citizenship and immigration law, corporate advisory and regulatory business matters.

His research on artificial intelligence in dispute resolution, The Digital Mediator, appears in an HEC-recognised policy journal, alongside an article on international arbitration in the Pakistan Law Journal. Two further works on AI and the Singapore Convention are expected in December 2026.

He has completed training programmes at LUMS and Harvard Law School (online) and is affiliated with CIArb, Young ICCA, Young SIAC, Young ICSID, the LCIA, WIPO ADR Young, the ABA and ASIL. He heads the ADR wings of several leading organisations, adjudicates national and international mediation and arbitration competitions, and is an accomplished guest speaker at ADR workshops and trainings.
